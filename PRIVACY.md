# Privacy Policy for Piko

**Last updated:** August 2026

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Piko ("the Extension") allows users to export conversations from Claude.ai. The Extension offers a hosted AI summarization feature (Capy AI) to process conversation contexts. When utilizing this hosted feature, the conversation data you choose to process is transmitted securely over HTTPS to our backend server. We do not persist, sell, or use your conversation data for any purposes other than providing the immediate context generation functionality.

If you use the "BYOK" (Bring Your Own Key) mode or standard raw Markdown export, your data is processed either locally in your browser or sent directly to the Gemini API using your personal API key.

## 2. Data Transmission Security
All data transmitted between the Extension and our backend server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS modern cryptography.

## 3. Data Retention
Our backend server processes the conversation data in memory to generate the requested context. We do not store or log your conversation content on our servers.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Extension relies on third-party APIs (such as Google's Gemini API) when generating contexts. Please refer to their respective privacy policies regarding how they handle the data transmitted through their APIs.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please refer to our GitHub repository: https://github.com/adityaraj/piko.
